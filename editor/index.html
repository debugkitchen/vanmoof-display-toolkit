Format & physical panel notes
20 rows × 9 columns, 3-bit intensity per pixel. The grid shows the physically absent corner LEDs as dashed circles — you can still draw on them, they just won't light up on real hardware. Animations use the frame-interleaved container format described in the main guide (section 4).
